The market opened beside the harbor every morning. Traders from the voyage sold
spice, cloth, and stories of the sea. The market crowd loved a voyage story
more than any spice.

Prices in the market changed with every ship. A hard voyage made spice rare and
the market loud. A calm voyage filled the market with cheap cloth and quiet
talk of the sea.

When the market closed, the harbor wall still held a few sailors. They planned
the next voyage and argued about the sea. The market would open again at dawn.
