The harbor woke slowly. Fishing boats left the harbor before sunrise and the
market beside the harbor filled with voices. Everyone in the harbor knew the
tide and trusted it.

A ship entered the harbor at noon, back from a long voyage. Children ran to the
harbor wall to see the ship. The voyage had been hard, the crew said, but the
harbor looked like mercy.

At dusk the harbor grew quiet. Lamps along the harbor wall marked the way home.
The ship would leave the harbor again, and another voyage would begin.
