The voyage began at dawn when the ship left the quiet harbor. The sea was calm
and the crew watched the horizon for any sign of land. A long voyage tests the
patience of every sailor, and this voyage would test them all.

On the third day the wind rose and the sea grew rough. The captain kept the
voyage on course, though the crew spoke of turning back. They had chosen this
voyage for the promise of a distant coast.

By the fifth day the sea settled again. Gulls followed the ship and the crew
believed the voyage was near its end. The harbor they dreamed of was still far,
but the voyage had become their home.
